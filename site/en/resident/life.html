Travel and Daily Life

This page directs people traveling in Chiba after the Obon holiday, and those having difficulty with utilities or shopping, to official information. Train service, road closures, and store hours change constantly. This page does not maintain a current list of route sections or store names. Always check the linked official source.

Trains and Buses

Landslides and flooding continue to affect some lines, including the Sotobo and Togane lines. Official operator guidance should also be your first source for alternative transport.
